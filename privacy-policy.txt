# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1/23/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Bot Catcher** (“the Bot”, “we”, “our”) collects, uses, and stores information.

## Information We Collect

Bot Catcher collects **minimal data** necessary for its operation:

* **Server ID** – used to identify the Discord server where the bot is installed
* **Server Configuration Settings**, including:

  * Whether compromised accounts should be **kicked or banned**
  * Whether affected users should be **re-invited** after removal

No other data is collected.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Bot Catcher **does not** collect, store, or process:

* Usernames or user IDs
* Message content
* IP addresses
* Email addresses
* Personal or sensitive data
* Logs of user activity beyond automated moderation actions

## How We Use the Data

Collected data is used **only** to:

* Apply server-specific moderation behavior
* Determine how the bot should respond when a compromised account is detected

## Data Storage and Security

* Data is stored securely and accessed only for bot functionality
* We do not sell, share, or transfer data to third parties

## Data Retention and Removal

* Server data is retained only while the bot remains in the server
* When the bot is removed, associated server data may be deleted

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
